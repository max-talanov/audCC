Attempt to extend L5 reverberation duration toward 0.5-2s via a second
lever (taur_l5e_rs: L5E regular-spiking cells' Ca2+-pool clearance,
giving SK2 adaptation time to accumulate across the reverberation and
terminate it, the same mechanism PYCellIB already uses at taur=500ms).

Baseline (best confirmed point): g_l5_rec=0.013, tau2_l5_rec=100,
g_i_e_l5=0.02, taur_l5e_rs=80 (default/unchanged) -- clean ~150-200ms
events, n_events=27, event_Hz=1.310, RE burst_size=7.68.

1) taur_l5e_rs=300, tau2_l5_rec=150: OVER-SUPPRESSED. Single L5E RS
   cell collapsed back to a bare 2-spike doublet (weaker than even the
   baseline). n_events=27, event_Hz=1.347 (looks similar in summary
   stats but total spikes 13174, much lower -- the reverberation was
   killed almost immediately by the added adaptation).

2) taur_l5e_rs=150, tau2_l5_rec=150: genuine EXTENSION at the single-
   cell level (3 volleys spanning ~515ms with a ~350ms clean gap after)
   -- but network-level LFP (out/ctx_nmda_taur150_local_lfp*.png) is
   BUSIER/less clean than baseline: n_events jumped to 36, event_Hz to
   1.773 (likely echo volleys being double-counted as separate events),
   L5 trace loses its flat baseline segments.

3) taur_l5e_rs=200, tau2_l5_rec=100 (isolating the adaptation effect
   alone, keeping the clean baseline NMDA decay): OVER-SUPPRESSED THE
   OTHER WAY. L5E RS cell now fires only ONE spike per cycle (not even
   a doublet) -- weaker than the unmodified baseline. n_events=26,
   event_Hz=1.303 (close to baseline in summary, but total spikes 11135
   vs baseline's 16621).

Conclusion: taur_l5e_rs and tau2_l5_rec interact NON-ADDITIVELY and
NON-MONOTONICALLY -- the same taur value that over-suppresses at
tau2=100 was part of a too-busy combination at tau2=150, and no single-
axis or simple joint adjustment tried so far beats the original
(0.013/100/80) point on both duration AND cleanliness simultaneously.
Manual one-at-a-time probing of this 2-parameter space is not
converging efficiently -- a genuine 2D grid sweep (or a proper
optimisation loop) is the honest next step if this is pursued further,
not more ad hoc single points.
